# A.T.S Guard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

A.T.S Guard ("the app") helps hosts of Among Us lobbies keep games fair by sending players back to their home screen if they open a blocked voice or chat app while the host has the game locked.

## What the app uses

**Your Among Us username.** You type it in when you first open the app. It is stored on your phone. When you connect to a host, the username is sent to that host's computer so they can see who is in the lobby. The host is another player, not us. We do not receive it.

**Which app just opened (Accessibility Service).** If you choose to turn on the Accessibility permission, the app is told the name of the app that has just come to the front of your screen. It uses this only to check whether that app is on the block list while the game is locked. It does not read the text, content, messages, passwords or anything else on your screen. The app name is not saved and is not sent anywhere.

**Host address and game phase.** You enter the host's IP address. The app connects to it to learn whether the game is in the lobby, in tasks or in a meeting. This connection goes only to the host you choose.

## What we do not do

- We do not run servers that collect your data, and we have no user accounts.
- We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
- We do not use ads or analytics, and we do not sell or share personal data with anyone.

## Your choices

You can turn off the Accessibility permission at any time in Settings, Accessibility. Uninstalling the app deletes the username stored on your phone. To have your username removed from a host's computer, ask the host.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new date will be shown at the top.
